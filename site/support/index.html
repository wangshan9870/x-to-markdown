Support / 使用帮助

Save a post / 保存单帖

Open an accessible X post and use the page panel, extension popup or Alt+Shift+X. Save to the library or export Markdown, HTML, PNG or an image ZIP. Refresh an already open X page after upgrading.

Sync your own bookmarks or likes / 同步自己的书签与点赞

Sign in to your own X account. Open the extension library, choose Sync Center, select Bookmarks or Likes and start. The import runs in the background without an open X tab; use the visible controls to pause, continue or stop. Other profiles’ likes and bookmark folders are unsupported. A rate-limited task can resume after the reset time; manually pausing or stopping cancels that retry. Interrupted imports retain a page checkpoint.

同步中心可在后台导入，无需保持 X 页面打开。支持暂停、继续、停止和增量同步；切换 X 账号后请停止旧任务再重新开始。限流任务会等待允许恢复的时间，手动暂停可取消恢复。

Replies and Articles / 回复与长文章

Save a reply after loading its ancestor posts to preserve available context. Open an X Article and wait for its body before saving; a preview alone cannot produce a complete article. Unavailable context is reported instead of invented.

Library and Obsidian / 文章库与Obsidian

Search and filter posts, select records for batch export, or use JSON backup and restoration. Select your own Obsidian vault to export notes; identical notes are skipped and conflicts are saved separately. Back up before removing browser data.

Report a problem / 问题反馈

Provide the extension and browser version, steps, error message and a redacted screenshot. Do not share Cookie, Token, passwords, private posts or account identifiers in public issues. / 提供版本、步骤、错误信息及脱敏截图，请勿公开凭据或私人帖子。
